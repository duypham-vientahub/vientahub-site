How the AI-powered build works

Three steps from your content to an AI in eLearning course

The AI-powered build is not a shortcut on quality. It moves the repetitive build work to Claude and keeps people in charge of structure, accuracy and the final check.

STEP 01

Organize the content

We collect and structure everything the course needs before a single screen is built.

  • Source documents, SME notes and approved script
  • Audio, voiceover and captions
  • Images, icons and your brand colors and fonts
STEP 02

Build HTML screens with Claude

Claude turns the content kit into interactive HTML files that follow one locked design system.

  • Custom interactions, animations and simulations
  • Narration and captions synced to on-screen text
  • Knowledge checks with tracked results
STEP 03

Insert into Storyline and publish

The HTML files go into Storyline 360 or Rise 360, then the course is tested and published.

  • Web objects linked to Storyline variables and navigation
  • Human QA on content, timing and accessibility
  • SCORM or xAPI package tested in your LMS
